# Code of conduct

## Our commitment

We are committed to a respectful, harassment-free project for everyone,
regardless of age, body size, disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics,
gender identity and expression, experience level, education, socioeconomic
status, nationality, personal appearance, race, caste, color, religion, or
sexual identity and orientation.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful, specific, and evidence-focused.
- Critique ideas and artifacts, not people.
- Distinguish observed facts, inferences, preferences, and uncertainty.
- Accept correction and take responsibility for mistakes.
- Protect private information and report security concerns privately.
- Avoid pressure, threats, harassment, sexualized conduct, discrimination,
  doxxing, or sustained disruption.

## Scope

This code applies in repository issues, pull requests, reviews, discussions,
project events, and public spaces where someone is representing Skeleton.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit or remove contributions and may warn, temporarily restrict,
or permanently ban participants whose behavior violates this code. Enforcement
should be proportionate, documented privately where appropriate, and should
protect reporters from retaliation.

Once private vulnerability reporting is enabled, conduct reports may be sent
through **Security -> Report a vulnerability** with `[conduct]` at the start of
the title. Do not include unrelated secret material. If that channel is not
available, use GitHub's platform reporting tools and do not post sensitive
details publicly.

Good-faith disagreement with a technical decision is not misconduct. Harassment,
identity-based attacks, disclosure of private information, and bad-faith
disruption are.
